# About Gentrep Academy

A guide for new members. Read it once — about ten minutes.

---

## What the Academy is

The Gentrep Academy is where you learn this business, one level at a time.

Every level is a short course: a few sessions you attend in person, and a scan at the door that records you were there.

You never tick your own boxes. That is what makes them worth something.

---

## The five levels

**Base Activation** — *everyone starts here*
Paperwork and five sessions. You learn what the product is, how the day works, and what you may and may not say. Finish it and My Team opens, so you can start bringing people in.

**Team Leader** — *lead generator*
Five classes on finding and inviting people, then one demonstration: bring three guests who check in, with a Platoon Leader watching.

**Squad Leader** — *presenter*
You learn to run the room yourself — presenting the product and the business to a group, not one person at a time.

**Platoon Leader** — *trains Team Leaders*
You learn to teach. This level is proven differently: it completes when a Team Leader you trained becomes certified.

**Company Commander** — *trains Squad Leaders*
The same again, one level up. You develop the people who develop the presenters.

Nobody skips a level. Each one teaches you a skill, then asks you to pass it down.

## How every course works

### 1. Some courses start with Admin and Compliance

Base Activation begins with four things to watch:

1. **Gutguard Dashboard Orientation** — where everything lives
2. **Distributor's Agreement** — what you are agreeing to
3. **Code of Ethics** — how we talk about the product, and about each other
4. **Gentrep Creed** — what we hold ourselves to

Watch the video first. The document opens after that. Then tap **I have read this and I agree**.

You can read the documents in **English or Tagalog**, and the app records which one you chose, along with your name, the date and the version. Read them properly — this is your protection, not just a requirement.

**You cannot book a seat until all four are done.** That is deliberate.

### 2. Pick your own dates

Every course lists what you need to attend. Tap **See dates** on any requirement and you will see every session posted for it — different Saturdays, different towns, with the time, the host and the seats remaining.

Choose the one you can actually reach. Then tap **Reserve**.

### 3. Show your pass at the door

Once you reserve, the button becomes **My pass**. Tap it for your QR code and show that when you arrive.

They scan it, and the requirement ticks off by itself. You do not mark yourself present — nobody can. That is what keeps every record honest, including yours.

### 4. Finish, and you are certified

When every requirement is met you get a certificate with your name, your level, the date and a reference number, and a button to share it with your team.

For Team Leader and above, you are then invited to a **Recognition Night** to be pinned.

## What each level asks of you

Courses are built from four kinds of requirement. You will see the type printed above each one.

| Type | What it means | How it is proven |
|---|---|---|
| **Watch and agree** | A video and a document | You agree in the app; the record is kept |
| **Attend** | A session you go to | The scan at the door |
| **Show it** | A demonstration | An upline watches and signs it off |
| **Earned by your trainee** | Someone you trained gets certified | Their certificate, not your word |

Base Activation uses the first two. Team Leader adds a demonstration. Platoon Leader and Company Commander are proven mostly by the people they train.

## If something changes

**Cannot make it?**
Open the date and tap cancel, then confirm. The seat goes back to someone else. Do it early if you can.

**Want a different date?**
Tap **Switch** on the one you prefer. There is no need to cancel first.

**Session full?**
It will say *Full — join the waitlist*. Tap **Waitlist** and you will be told when a seat opens.

**Missed one?**
The row turns red and says *Missed — pick another date*. Nothing else you have done is lost. Just choose again.

**No dates posted yet?**
It will say so. That one is waiting on us, not on you. You will be notified when a date appears.

**Is there a deadline?**
No. Most people finish a level in about three weeks, but nothing expires and nothing is taken away. Take the time you need.

## Reading the screen

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| Gold tick | Done, and proven by a scan |
| Gold ring | Booked, not attended yet |
| Red mark | Missed — pick another date |
| Blue button | Something for you to tap |
| The chain on the left | Your progress through the course, top to bottom |

At the bottom of the screen there is always one line: **Next**. If you are unsure what to do, tap that.

## Questions

**Can I skip a class?**
No. All of them, in whatever order suits you — but all of them.

**I lost my phone. Did I lose my progress?**
No. Everything is on your record, not on your device. Just log in again.

**Why can't I tick my own boxes?**
Because then a tick would mean nothing. Your record is worth something precisely because you cannot fake it.

**Who can see my progress?**
You, and your sponsor. That is how they know when to help you.

**Where do I ask for help?**
The **Team chat** button at the top opens your Telegram group. Ask where the venue is, whether anyone is travelling from your area, what to wear. No question is a stupid one — it is better to ask than to miss the session.

---

## One last thing

The app only keeps the record. What you actually learn happens in the room — from the people, the stories, and the questions you ask there.

So book the date and go.
